The Mac mini’s display was locked, so these are native content-view renders from an isolated Float fixture. The system appearance was switched while each window stayed open. The fixture kept the panel at its expanded size and allowed opening the pickers while the display was locked. The original system appearance was restored. No prompts were sent.
